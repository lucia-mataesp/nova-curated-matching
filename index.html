How it works

Three steps, no guesswork.

1

Tell us who you'd like to meet

A few questions about your own background, and about the kind of person and conversation you're looking for.

2

We find someone relevant

We look across the Nova community for people who actually match what you're after — not just a keyword, a genuinely relevant fit.

3

We make the introduction

If it looks like a good match, we reach out personally to both of you. The connection only happens if you're both interested.

Why we're doing this

We tried this before. It worked.

In 2024, Nova ran a version of this by hand: members told us who they wanted to meet, and the team searched for a match and made the introduction personally. It worked — almost everyone who was introduced actually connected — because it wasn't random, and it wasn't automated spam.

We're bringing that back, this time helping more people at once — but keeping the part that made it work: a real, curated match, and an introduction made with care.
